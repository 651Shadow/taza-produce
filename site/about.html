    Since 1988

    A family market on 86th Street.

    Tazza Produce began as a small neighborhood grocery and grew into a Bay Ridge staple. Three decades later, it is still run by the same family.

    Our story

    From one shelf to a 24-hour market

    What started with a few crates of produce became a full Middle Eastern and halal grocery. We kept the things that matter: fresh stock every day, halal meats you can trace, and a counter where we know our regulars by name.

    Being open 24 hours means the early cook, the night-shift worker, and the parent grabbing pitas at midnight all have a place to shop.

    Our promise
